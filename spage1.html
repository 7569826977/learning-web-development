    Synopsis

                Better Call Saul is a Breaking Bad prequel that follows Jimmy McGill,
            a struggling lawyer trying to make a name for himself in Albuquerque, New Mexico.
            As he transforms into the morally flexible Saul Goodman, he gets entangled with criminals, drug cartels, and personal conflicts.
            The series explores his descent into corruption and the events leading up to Breaking Bad.
            It also delves into the lives of key characters like Mike Ehrmantraut and Kim Wexler.
            A story of ambition, identity, and consequence.
